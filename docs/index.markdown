I am a theoretical and computational astrophysicist working on broad topics related to galaxy formation and evolution, the interstellar medium, and galactic halos, with an emphasis on understanding the "non-thermal" physics of magnetism and cosmic ray transport & feedback. I use a variety of analytical and state-of-the-art simulation methods deeply anchored to interpretation and prediction of multi-wavelength, multi-tracer observables to uncover how multi-scale, non-thermal physics shape galaxies across cosmic time.
