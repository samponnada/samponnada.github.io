<img src="sam_bryce.jpeg" alt="sam" width="200" align="left" style="padding-right: 15px; float: left;"> I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Astronomy and Plasma Physics division at the Chalmers University of Technology in Sweden. I completed my PhD in Astrophysics at the California Institute of Technology in the summer of 2025, before continuing work there as a Research Scientist in Theoretical Astrophysics. I use theory and computation to probe broad topics related to galaxy formation and evolution, the interstellar medium, and galactic halos, with an emphasis on understanding the "non-thermal" physics of magnetism and cosmic ray transport & feedback. I use a variety of analytical and state-of-the-art simulation methods deeply anchored to interpretation and prediction of multi-wavelength, multi-tracer observables to uncover how multi-scale, non-thermal physics shape galaxies across cosmic time.
